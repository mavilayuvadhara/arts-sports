## 🤝 About Us

Welcome to **Mavila Yuvadhara**! We are a community of good friends dedicated to influence positively in the society and for better social life through arts and sports.

Our mission is to provide a supportive space where members can collaborate on projects, share knowledge, and grow together.

---

### 🎯 Our Mission & Vision
* **Connect:** Build a strong network of like-minded individuals.
* **Learn:** Host weekly workshops, hackathons, and guest speaker events.
* **Build:** Collaborate on open-source projects that solve real-world problems.

### 📅 When & Where We Meet
* **Days:** Every first Saturday and Sunday of Every Month there will be general body meeting**
* **Time:** 6:00 PM – 7:30 PM (EST)
