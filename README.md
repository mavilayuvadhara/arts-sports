### 🤝 Welcome to Our Club!
We are a community dedicated to learning, collaborating, and growing together. 

* **Our Mission:** To provide a supportive space for project building and skill sharing.
* **Meetings:** Every First Saturday and Sunday on each month at 6:00 PM, there will be a general body meeting.
* **Who can join:** Anyone! No prior experience is required.

</details>
### 🚀 How to Join
1. Star this repository to stay updated.
2. Attend our next general meeting!
